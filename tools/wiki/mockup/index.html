    The game frame is the chosen resolution fitted into the browser, its canvases drawn at the resolution's own pixels; every length in the window is in units of the frame's height / 720. The window, its tree, the entry and its demos come from art/wiki/; what the stage shows comes from sim.js; the kit's parts from art/wiki/kit/, the text styles from the replay HUD's art/hud/layout.json. URL switches: res=, size=, mode=sp|mp, mod=, skin=kit|whitebox, rewind=0|1, open, entry=, demo= (from 1), try, gfx=whitebox|game (the stage in 3D, as the game draws it, from the packs tools/wiki/gfx.py resolves from the local install into the gitignored gfx/), ss= (its supersampling, default 2).
