Render options — the drop-down

Every choice settled, in one place. The panel starts closed, the way a drop-down does: click the sprocket at the right of the top bar to open it, the sprocket again or anywhere else on the frame to dismiss it. Clicking a row leaves it open. The magnified copy beside the frame ignores all of that, so the panel stays there to be looked at.

Six gadgets, no pages, hanging off the top bar at the right: panel id=0 at (704,32) 304×212. The stage buttons are the engine's own art from anims/commongui.gaf — extracted, never tracked, run tools/guiart.py first. The ground is frontend.gaf's own back* nine-slice, the shell's mottled panelling, composed at 304×212 by tools/guipanel.py; only the seven recesses are drawn over it, because no stock frame has seven h20 wells and none puts a label beside its control instead of above it.

No Apply. A stage button is the setting, so there is no edit buffer for an Apply to commit: a row writes its key on the click, and the panel is dismissed by the trigger or by clicking away. That takes 40 px off the panel and an eighth gadget out of the .GUI.

The trigger has no plate — the register of mainmenu.gui's Credits gadget, which is an ordinary id=1 button with text= empty and attribs=1026 where every other button on that screen is attribs=2. Its ink walks TA's palette ramp 55..63 the way the Cavedog logo's does, and the hover and press states are a slide along that ramp rather than a second picture.

The menu never offers the unmodified engine — the only question a row asks is which of our two renderers owns it. Owner's rule · 2026-09-08
