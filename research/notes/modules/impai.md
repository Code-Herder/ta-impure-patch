# ImpAI in the engine

ImpAI's seam, its seat table, the seat choice `seatai` and the SKIRMISH seat button that writes it, what each of its seats knows and the self-checks that hold that to the engine, the per-type threat table, the type information and the build menus, the seat's own site test, the debug overlay and the cost instrument, the Difficulty handicap's exemption and the console's `AI` command (`tagpu_impai.c`, `tagpu_impai.h`, `tagpu_skirmish.c`; the engine-free half `impai.h`, `impai_know.c`, `impai_maps.c`, `impai_tinfo.c` and the brain `impai_brain.c`, `impai_econ.c`, `impai_build.c`; the engine's reads `tagpu_worldq.c`). ImpAI plays the seats it is given -- what each knows, and the brain's economy: its builders, its factories, its expansion to metal -- and takes them out of the handicap; the stock AI plays every other computer seat (*The seam and the wrapper*, *The brain*). The plan, the engine facts it rests on and what ImpAI will do are [entering the game](../impai/engine-seam.html) and [ImpAI for the player](../impai/player.html).

*A module note: it states the module as it is now. A landing that changes the module edits its section here in place; what the landing measured and what its review found go to the commit, its `landing-review:` note and the gate's plan note.*

Addresses are VAs for our pristine build (ImageBase `0x400000`). `main` = `*(void**)0x511DE8`; the player records are at `main+0x1B63`, stride `0x14B`, ten of them; `+0x73` is the controller (1 human, 2 AI, 3 remote or a replay puppet).

## The seat choice: `seatai`

`impure.cfg`'s key `seatai` is `none`, or the seats ImpAI plays as digits 0–9 separated by commas (`seatai=1,3`): bit i for settings entry i, which is record i on every path that seats a skirmish ([sim](../engine/sim.html), *Seating a level*). Anything else logs `settings: seatai: <text> not understood - no seat plays ImpAI` and reads as none. `tagpu_settings_seatai` answers it under `tagpu_defaults.off` too, as the engine honours its own SKIRMISH registry keys under every launch. The DLL writes it from one place, the seat button below (`tagpu_settings_set_seatai`, under every launch too, so a click under `tagpu_defaults.off` rewrites `impure.cfg` from the values read at attach); tacli and the player write it too. **Every tacli launch writes it** (`write_harness_store`, and the remote launch): the seats a scenario names `impai` (`tacli scenario load`) or `tacli launch --seatai 1,3` names, or `none`, so no launch plays an earlier launch's seats ([the scenario format](../scenario-format.html)).

**The seat button** (`tagpu_skirmish.c`): on the SKIRMISH screen a computer seat takes two steps, `OTA AI` then `ImpAI`, so `Player<N>` cycles **Open → OTA AI → ImpAI → Player** (when no seat is the player, the engine's own rule) **→ Open**. The engine's controller value stays 2 for both; the click sets or clears the seat's `seatai` bit and writes the label. Two call-site redirects, byte-matched at attach, both or neither, and neither when ImpAI is unavailable (the screen is then stock): `0x47B178`, the handler's call of the cycle `0x4797E0` — a click on a seat at its first computer step moves it to the second without the cycle, every other click runs the cycle, a seat entering Computer takes the first step and one leaving it loses its bit — and `0x47A1FC`, the screen init's label call for a Computer seat, which writes `ImpAI` or `OTA AI` by the bit. The seat is bounded below 10 and the screen's seat count `main+0x38D81`, else the click is stock's. `IMPAI_FIRST_COMPUTER` (0: OTA AI first) is the one constant that orders the two steps. Attach logs `skirmish: the seat button steps a computer seat through OTA AI and ImpAI (0x47B178, 0x47A1FC)` or why it stays stock ([ui](../engine/ui.html), *The SKIRMISH screen's handler*).

**A saved game does not carry the choice.** The save holds the records' controllers, not the seat table, so a reloaded game seats ImpAI from the `seatai` in force when it loads, and the seats its level-init line names can differ from the game that was saved. The save's players restore `0x466050` also writes `+0x73` (`0x4662C3`) after the level init, so the line can name a seat that is not controller 2 in play; every reader re-tests controller 2, so such a mark does nothing.

## The install

At attach, after `tagpu_reclaim_init` and `tagpu_replay_init`, whatever the levers, and only when the level's two edges are ours:

- replay's play sites are in (`tagpu_replay_level_init_in`), among them the level init's `PLAYERS` call `0x4919C8`, retargeted to `tagpu_replay_level_players`, the only caller of ImpAI's level init;
- the teardown wrap `0x491B60` is in (`tagpu_reclaim_level_tracked`), the only caller of ImpAI's level end.

Then it compares twenty sites with their stock bytes: the seam `0x465031`, the `AI` command's two calls `0x4162F4` and `0x4162FE`, the sixteen handicap sites and the site test's `0x47D3D1`. Only when every one matches does it build the stubs and the seat's site-test thunk, in one `SIM_CODE` page, and write all twenty, putting back every site it wrote if a write fails; only then does `WQ_SITE_SEAT` get the thunk. Success logs `impai: seam 0x465031, the AI command 0x4162F4 and 0x4162FE, 16 handicap sites and the seat's site test 0x47D3D1 in; seats at 0x<addr>, handicap counts at 0x<addr>` (the addresses `handicap_check.py` peeks). Otherwise nothing is written, ImpAI is unavailable, and the attach and every level log `impai: unavailable: <why>`, a mismatch as `<VA> not stock (<bytes>)`. The sites' rows are in [the hook map](hook-map.html), *The AI seat*.

**The test levers** are read at attach by `tagpu_impai_levers`, before the simulation registry closes its lever list, so a recording lists each: `tagpu_impai_check.on` turns on the self-checks (*The self-checks*, below), `tagpu_impaiov.on` the overlay (*The overlay*), `tagpu_impai_budget.on` the cost run's raised budgets (*The cost*). With it, once every site above is in, the install also retargets the radar pass's call `0x46556D` (`E8 CE 1E 00 00`, byte-matched) and logs `impai: check: line of sight in every ImpAI turn, radar at 0x46556D`, or why the radar check is out. Without it `0x46556D` stays stock.

## The seam and the wrapper

The players phase's `call 0x408C40` at `0x465031` calls `impai_ai_tick`, `__fastcall(obj, edx)`: ECX arrives as its first argument and it pops nothing, so the site's stack is unchanged, and `EBX`, `ESI`, `EDI` and `EBP`, which the players phase uses after the call, are callee-saved. The AI object's seat is ImpAI's (`impai_seat_marked`) when all of these hold:

- **the bound**: `rec = *(obj+0)` lies at `main+0x1B63 + i·0x14B`, `i` below 10, by range and exact division, before anything is read through it;
- the record is seated with controller 2, the test `0x408C40` makes before it plans;
- the seat table marks record `i`, and `i` is not a puppet seat.

Any other seat takes the stock tick `0x408C40`, and the recorder's observer on its entry sees the call. **An ImpAI seat never runs `0x408C40`'s group assignment `0x408830` or its ten planners**, on either thread: of the tick it runs only what both its exits end with for an active type-2 record, the weapon scan `0x4089A0(obj, 1)` (`0x408C96..0x408C9A`), called by address so that the extra-weapons module's detour of its entry runs. On the **loader thread's** one players phase (`0x497C10`) that is all it runs. On the **game thread** (`GetCurrentThreadId()` against `g_ddraw.gui_thread_id`) the seat's turn -- its observation (*What a seat knows*), then the brain on its decision tick (*The brain*) -- and the scan run inside `tagpu_rec_ai_turn_enter`/`_leave`, the recorder's attribution bracket beside `ai_enter`: the orders the turn builds are the seat's in the recorder's AI activity window, and the scan's detour ends the attribution before it scans (`tagpu_rec_ai_planning_end`), as for the stock tick.

## What a seat knows

Each ImpAI seat keeps, for every unit slot, what it may know of the unit there: `s_known[slot].seat[i]`, 8 bytes (`impai.h`, `IMPAI_SEEN`): the type and owner last identified (`0xFFFF` and `0xFF` when never), the classes it holds the slot by now (`how`: own, line of sight, shared radar, radar, sonar), and the 32-px block last known. An all-zero row is empty. **The information rule**: a row changes only through what the seat observes, so a death or a slot reuse the seat cannot see changes nothing it knows, and no engine fact reaches a decision through a row. A row with no class left is a **ghost**: the last thing known, kept at its block until the seat sees that block again, when it is emptied.

**One turn** (`impai_observe`, then the engine-free `impai_know_tick` over the engine's WORLDQ, `tagpu_worldq.c`), at the seat's place in the players phase, after the step's unit tick, projectiles and explosions and before the seat's own sight emits, so the view is the start of the seat's turn at this tick; nothing after the players phase in the step writes a position, the alive bit, an owner, a type or cloak ([sim](../engine/sim.html), the step):

1. **The line-of-sight slice**: `OBS_SLICE` (2048) slots from the seat's cursor, wrapping. `observe` answers the first class that holds: **own** (the owner byte is the seat); **line of sight**, `0x465AC0`'s rule for the seat — not cloaked, not submerged, then the four points of the unit's box ([map](../engine/map.html), `0x465AC0`) at the live `fix_los_shear` row, read in the seat's counters under True line of sight and in the mapped grid **with the seat's own bit** `rec+0x146` otherwise (the engine's own reads take the viewed player's bit whatever record asks; the stamp writes every record's); **shared**, loop 1 of the radar pass for the seat (the owner's record allies the seat and shares radar) — never in a skirmish, where ShareRadar cannot be set. Own and line of sight identify the type and owner; shared gives the block alone. **A submerged unit is never identified**: the engine sees one only by `+0x110` bit 9, the viewed player's sonar; the seat knows it through its own sonar.
2. **The radar pass**: started when idle and `GameTime % 30 == (3·seat + 1) % 30`, so two seats never start on one tick, and continued every tick until it completes, at most `RADAR_VISITS` (32768) target visits a tick, rounded up to the end of the emitter or jammer in progress. It is `0x467440`'s five loops for the seat ([recorder](../engine/recorder.html), `0x467440`; loop 4, the same for every viewer, left out), walking the engine's own bucket chains as `0x47E890` does, since a unit's bucket can lag its position and a carried unit is in none. Its answer, a nibble a slot in `s_radar_bits[seat]` (radar, sonar, jammed, kept), is published into the rows when it completes: a contact takes the radar and sonar classes, and the block when no class of now holds it; a slot the pass did not find loses them. Under load a pass grows staler; a tick's work does not grow.
3. **The patch scan**: `CLU_ROWS` (16) map rows a tick through the game's own patch rule, `tasim_snap_patches` — the explored metal patches Quick Build tells apart, each flood at most `SNAP_PATCH_CAP` cells and the tick's at most `CLU_FLOOD` (`2 · SNAP_PATCH_CAP`) — into the second half of the seat's `s_cluster[seat]` (2 × 256 `SNAP_PATCH`), which replaces the first, published half at the last row, so a half-done scan is never read. A row whose floods need more than one tick's budget is passed over with its patches unreported, so the scan always advances. The flood's arena is `tagpu_sim_scratch`, freed before the turn returns.

**The reach labels** (`impai_reach_build`, `SIM_CONTENT`): at every level init, live or puppet, on the loader thread inside PLAYERS, after `0x440940` has built the movement classes' pathing maps ([map](../engine/map.html), the pathing maps), each class the level has is labelled by 4-connectivity over its passable cells (code not 0) and each 32-px block takes the label of its first passable cell, 0 when none: one `u16` a block, class after class, in one block sized for the level's classes (`s_reach`, `s_reach_ix`) and freed at the next level init. A class whose map is not the PLOT grid's size is read as blocked everywhere. Units are not obstacles (none exists at PLAYERS); a saved game's features, restored after this point, are missing. The level init logs `impai: reach classes=<n> blocks=<w>x<h> components=<n> cells=<n> blocks_written=<n> us=<n>`: the work in counts (each class labels every cell once and reads its four neighbours, then writes every block) and, as a measurement, its time. MEASURED 2026-11-12 on Comet Catcher (384 × 480 cells, 15 classes, the harness's worker): 2 764 800 cells labelled and 691 200 blocks written in 21 ms. The work is linear in cells × classes, so a 2048 × 2048 map with 15 classes is about 0.5 s and 30 MB of labels, with 32 classes about 1 s and 64 MB [INFERRED by scaling, not run]; no bound beyond the classes' W and H holds it.

**The threat table** (`s_threat_def`, `SIM_CONTENT`, one 16-byte `IMPAI_THREAT` a unit type, `TAGPU_LIM_TYPES` rows, 256 KB): at every level init, live or puppet, on the loader thread inside `0x4919C8`, `tagpu_worldq_role_in` reads every type's classifier input where the unit-data load `0x42D2E0` (`0x4918CA`) left it, and `tasim_roles_classify` classifies it by the rule `tasim_run roles` applies to the install's files ([the calibration runner](tasim-calibration.html), *Roles*); `impai_threat_from_roles` rounds damage per second against ground, air and water, the ground range, the value, the layer group and the role flags into the row. The input is the file filler's keys from where the engine keeps them ([keys](../engine/keys.html)): the def array below UNITINFOCount (flags `+0x241`/`+0x245` masked to the bits the classifier tests, `bmcode`, the costs, `maxdamage`, `workertime`, `maxvelocity`, the sensor words, the water depths `+0x1BE`/`+0x1C0`, `energyuse`, `makesmetal`, `transportcapacity`); `weapon1`..`weapon10` through the extra-weapons module, a slot whose record is the no-weapon entry left empty, each weapon's range `+0xDC`, reload `+0xE4`, burst `+0xEA`, default damage `+0xD4`, per-shot costs and flags `+0x111`, and Impure's `nottoair` from the weapon keys; the air damage, each weapon's mean over the types of category `VTOL` of its `[DAMAGE]` row for the type, else its default ([weapons](../engine/weapons.html), the table's layout); and each slot 0..2's bad-target tag, the name of the category-map entry whose mask the def holds at `+0x231 + 4k`, with NOTAIR among its words. The category map is walked, never looked up through `0x488C50`, which inserts on a miss ([units](../engine/units.html), *The unit-category map*). The classifier's −1 (no commander, so no tech level) leaves every figure the row takes defined; a level whose input cannot be had has no table, and the maps draw no threat or influence. The level init logs `impai: threat types=<n>`; under the check lever also every type's role and figures, which `tools/impai/roles_check.py` compares with `tasim_run roles` type by type: equal on the retail install (278 types, MEASURED 2026-11-12 on Comet Catcher), the tech level apart (the engine filler gives no build options) and type 0, the engine's reserved type, which the log names `None` and no FBI defines.

**The type information** (`s_tinfo`, `SIM_CONTENT`, one 24-byte `IMPAI_TINFO` a unit type): built beside the threat table from the same classification, by the engine-free `impai_tinfo_build` over the **content view** (`tagpu_worldq_open_content`: the defs and the map, no live unit, every site refused, so it opens on the loader thread, live or puppet). A type's category is the first that holds: commander (the classifier's flag), extractor or geothermal (its kind), factory (the classifier's flag), constructor (a mobile builder with a worker time), maker (a building with `makesmetal` and `energyuse` above 0), energy (an unarmed building whose energy gain is above 0), other; its gains are a 30-tick pass's on this map (the wind's mean and the tide from the map: the loader body writes the wind's divisor at `0x4918ED` and calls the wind step at `0x491903`, both before `PLAYERS`); its value is metal plus energy over the classifier's ratio. The level init logs `impai: tinfo mex=<n> energy=<n> maker=<n> factory=<n> cons=<n>`, or `impai: tinfo none: …` when the view cannot be opened.

**The build menus** (`s_menu`, `s_menu_pool`, `SIM_CONTENT`): for each side an ImpAI seat of the level plays, every factory's, constructor's and commander's build menu, read by Quick Build's page rule (`tagpu_resource.c`, `tagpu_buildmenu_types`, on the loader thread after the defs and the download records are loaded) with every type a press reaches: the sidebar arms a type whose def `+0x22F` (`bmcode`) is 0 (`0x41AB7F`) and hands any other to the factory's queue (`0x41AC3C`, `0x419B00`; [ui](../engine/ui.html), the build press), and a factory's menu holds only the second kind, so Quick Build's own rule, the armed types alone, leaves every factory's menu empty and no plant can be chosen, sorted by (side, builder) into 2048 rows and a 65 536-type pool. The sides are the marked seats' (PlayerInfo `+0x95`, bounded below `main+0x37F39` and 5) on a live level, the recording header's ImpAI seats' on a puppet (`tagpu_replay_seat_ai`). All or none: past either table the level init logs `impai: menus past their tables (…)`, the level has no menus and marks no seat. Each side logs `impai: menus side=<s> builders=<n> types=<n> ms=<t>`. `tagpu_impai_menu` is ImpAI's `WqMenuFn`: the seat's side, a binary search, a copy.

**The maps** (`impai_maps.c`, `impai_maps_build`), engine-free and derived on demand, never stored: over one seat's rows, its patches, the threat table and the map size, at 256-px cells (at most 128 × 128) — **threat** per layer (ground, air, water), each identified enemy row's damage per second stamped on the cells whose centres lie within its type's range of its block's centre; **influence**, own value minus identified enemies', summed over each cell's 3 × 3 box; **presence**, enemy rows known only by radar or as ghosts. Only the overlay builds them in the game yet.

The knowledge (`s_known`, keyed by unit slot, `TAGPU_PK_DESIGN_SLOTS` rows of 80 bytes), each seat's cursors and pass (`s_seatst`), the passes' bits (`s_radar_bits`) and the patches (`s_cluster`) are `SIM_STATE`, cleared at every level init; a puppet's come from its recording. The work counters are `SIM_PRIVATE`: each level end logs `impai: work seat=<i> ticks=<n> refused=<n> slots_max=<n>/<bound> visits_max=<n>/<bound> cells_max=<n>/<bound> flood_max=<n>/<bound> scans=<n> clusters=<n>`, a tick's largest count against its bound (the slice plus a pass's publish, one observe a slot; the budget plus one walk, which reads each slot's unit once at most; the scan's rows across the map; the flood budget), the patch scan's completed passes and the patches published last. `refused` counts turns whose view or flood arena could not be had (a bound of `tagpu_worldq_open` failed).

## The self-checks

Under `tagpu_impai_check.on` (`SIM_PRIVATE` counts, logged at the level end):

- **Line of sight**, in every ImpAI turn: the rule against the engine's `0x465AC0` for every live unit of another owner that is not submerged, at one instant on one thread. Under True line of sight for the seat's own record (`impai: check los seat=<i> evaluated=<n> mismatch=<n>`); in the other modes, where `0x465AC0` reads the viewed player's bit whatever record it is given, for the local record `main[0x2A43]`, where both read the same bit (`impai: check los-local …`).
- **The seat's site test**, in every ImpAI turn: four sites of the level's smallest plain building type, two near the seat's first unit and two anywhere, tested through the thunk (`WQ_SITE_SEAT`) and held to the seat's own mapped bit at the block `0x47D2E0` reads (`tagpu_worldq_site_mapped`); the same site through the stock test counts where the two answers differ (`impai: check site evaluated=<n> mismatch=<n> accepted=<n> stock_differs=<n>`).
- **Radar**, at `0x46556D`: the engine's pass for the viewed record, then ImpAI's copy for the same record, unbudgeted, and every live unit's bits 8 and 9 compared — bit 10 too where loop 1 cleared it (`impai: check radar passes=<n> evaluated=<n> mismatch=<n>`).

- **The patches' evidence**, at the level end: each observing seat's published patches (`impai: patch seat=<i> ax az x0 z0 x1 z1 cells`) and the map's metal as the patch rule reads it -- the feature grid's byte `+7` of every cell and the threshold game state `+0xD30` gives the open, held to 255 -- run-length coded (`impai: metal ...`). MEASURED 2026-11-12: on Comet Catcher (384 × 480 cells, threshold 3) each seat completed 186 scans and published 81 patches, every one of them among the 81 the whole map holds; on Core Prime Industrial Area the threshold is 255, so no cell is above it and 136 completed scans publish none, which the rule requires.

The first mismatch of each is logged with its slot. `tools/impai/impai_check.py logs --los true|permanent [--puppet <log>]... [--patches] <logs>` judges a game's lines: (a) every check `mismatch=0`, at least 10 000 line-of-sight comparisons (each seat's under True, the local record's under Permanent) and 50 radar passes; (b) with the puppet's logs, the overlay digests (*The overlay*); (c) every `work` line within its bounds with `refused=0` and at least one completed patch scan; (d) a `cost` line for every seat; (e) every published patch is one the patch rule finds on the whole map's metal, recomputed with every cell explored (a closed patch's flood saw every cell it needed explored, so it is the whole map's), and with `--patches` at least one was published.

Without the lever too, each ImpAI seat logs once a level `impai: econ seat=<i> t=<n> pass=<p> metal energy metal_max energy_max metal_in energy_in metal_out energy_out`: what the economy op reads of its record, at its first decision tick from GameTime 590 (`GameTime % 15 == 7·seat % 15`) at which the record's next 30-tick block is not due in that step (`pass`, the record's `+0xF0`, above GameTime). The turn at `0x465031` comes before the block's test at `0x46507D` in the same iteration of the players phase, and the economy pass runs only inside the block, so a peek held at the end of any step from `t` to `pass − 1` reads the same fields, whatever the seat's phase. `tools/impai/econ_check.py <instance> [--scenario …]` holds a launch, steps one tick at a time from 589 and compares each seat's line with one peek of its record taken at the line's own tick, bit for bit.

## The seat's own site test

`0x47D2E0` refuses a site whose footprint centre is unmapped, and the mapped bit it tests is the **viewed** player's, `1 << main+0x2A43` (`mov cl,[ebx+0x2A43]` at `0x47D3D1`), whatever record it is passed ([orders](../engine/orders.html), `0x47D2E0`). ImpAI calls it through a thunk in its stubs' page — four `push [esp+0x10]` and `call 0x47D2E0`, `ret 0x10` — and `0x47D3D1` jumps to a stub that compares the function's return address `[esp+0x3C]` (`sub esp,0x2C` and four pushes in, `0x47D2E0..0x47D2E7`) with the thunk's: equal, it loads the passed record's own bit `[edi+0x146]` (EDI is the record from `0x47D358`); otherwise the stock instruction. Either way it resumes at `0x47D3D7`. Flags need not survive: the `add` at `0x47D3EE` rewrites them before the `je` at `0x47D3FA`. **The invariant is an identity, not a state**: only a call through the thunk returns to it, so every stock caller (`0x40A4CD`, `0x4198C6`, `0x47DBE5`, the only three) reads `main+0x2A43` as before, and no jump lands inside `0x47D3D2..0x47D3D6`. `tagpu_worldq_open(..., WQ_SITE_SEAT, ...)` opens a view whose `site_ok` calls the thunk; it refuses until the install has written every site.

## The brain

`impai_brain_decide` (`impai.h`, the brain) is one decision for one seat over a WORLDQ: engine-free, with no statics, compiled on SSE2 like the shared core. In the game it runs in the seat's turn when `impai_brain_due` holds (`GameTime % 15 == 7·seat % 15`, so no two seats decide on one tick), over the seat's `WQ_SITE_SEAT` view with ImpAI's build menus, the reach labels and the seat's own rows for the threat map (`impai_decide`); between decisions it does no work. Its state is `SIM_STATE`: `s_brain`, one `IMPAI_BRAIN` a seat (the anchor, the fail ring, the explorer), and `s_job`, one `IMPAI_JOB` a unit slot for whichever seat owns it -- a job holds only while its slot holds a live unit of the seat's of the job's type, and a slot reused by the same type inherits it -- both cleared at every level init and a puppet's restored from its recording. Its scratch, and the snap's, are static `SIM_TRANSIENT` buffers written before they are read, so a turn allocates nothing. The level end logs, per seat that decided, `impai: eco seat=<i> decisions orders queued failed mex energy makers factories cons metal_in energy_in` (the most units of each category any census counted, frames included -- the most, because a fixture's end scenario clears every unit before the level ends --, and the record's last pass) and `impai: work seat=<i> decisions refused census_max=<n>/<block> sites_max snaps_max roots_max queue_max`, each budget's largest use in one decision against the level row's bound, which `impai_check.py` (c) judges. A decision runs, in order: the census over the seat's unit block (counts by category, idle builders by the stock planner's test — no head order or a head with `0x4000` —, claims, unfinished buildings, the factories' queued constructors), the anchor and the base box, the job checks (a job done, failed into the fail ring, or stopped past `job_timeout`), the economy's stalls and projections, the ladder for each idle builder up to `assign_max` (a stall → energy; the opening factory, the least value among the plants whose menu offers a constructor that can build an extractor, a water-only plant (`IMPAI_TF_WATER`) only on a wet anchor as for energy -- CORE's shipyard CORSY, 612.5, is the least of its four plants and fits no site of a dry map; a free patch → extractor; energy to keep up; a maker; one explorer; assist), and the factories' constructor queues. Sites come from `impai_build.c`: a patch free of claims and failures, reachable on the builder's class (the reach labels) and under no ground threat, scored in integers and placed by the snap (`tasim_snap_site`) — a constructor explores only when no such patch is left for it —; the base ring (`place_ring` around the base box, `gap` cells apart, off metal); the explore cell; the assist. ## The overlay

The test lever `tagpu_impaiov.on`, `<seat>[:<layer>]` with the layer `ground`, `air`, `water`, `influence`, `presence` or `reach` (default `ground`), shows what one seat knows; `tacli arm <instance> impaiov.on=1:air` writes it. Never a player setting. A seat shows while ImpAI plays it or its knowledge generation is above 0, so a puppet shows the seat its recording played.

- **The fill** (`tagpu_impai_overlay`), from the frame packet's publisher on the game thread: one `PK_IMPAIHDR` (seat, layer, radar age = GameTime less the last published pass, knowledge generation) and at most 16 384 `PK_IMPAI` of 16 bytes (`TAGPU_PK_TRUNC_IMPAI` when cut) -- the layer's nonzero 256-px cells, shaded 1 + floor(log2 value) held to 15 (influence carries its sign; reach the label of each cell's centre block for the level's lowest movement class), then every row in slot order (own, contact, ghost, with its classes), then the published patches. It reads ImpAI's declared state and content, and of the engine only what the recording carries too -- GameTime, the map's size, the seat's alliance row -- and the ground's height `0x485070` at each point; so a puppet's overlay is the live game's.
- **The maps' cache** (`SIM_PRIVATE`, about 420 KB): rebuilt in the first fill of each 30-tick epoch (GameTime / 30) in which the knowledge generation moved, and in a level's first fill; the level init forgets it. Under the harness's turbo every tick has its fill, so the live game and its puppet rebuild at the same GameTimes.
- **The draw** (`tagpu_mark.c`, `gather_impai`), after the reclaim sweep, fog off: a cell as a filled square centred in it, its side growing with the shade; a row as a 4-px square -- own blue, line of sight red, shared radar cyan, radar or sonar yellow, a ghost grey; a patch as a ring; the readout `IMPAI SEAT 1 - GROUND - RADAR AGE 12`. The colours are the standard palette's system entries 247..254.
- **The digests**, under the check lever: each rebuild logs `impai: ov seat=<i> tick=<t> known=<FNV of the seat's rows and patches> ov=<FNV of the maps, the reach cells and the items> items=<n>`. `impai_check.py` (b) pairs a live game's lines with its puppet's: every known digest both logged must map to the same overlay digest, at least 20 of them, and within one game a known digest never maps to two.

## The cost

`QueryPerformanceCounter` around each turn -- the views' opens, the knowledge tick and the decision, not the self-checks nor the weapon scan -- kept per seat as the largest, the sum and a log2 histogram of microseconds (`SIM_PRIVATE`); the level end logs `impai: cost seat=<i> ticks=<n> max_us=<n> mean_us=<x> p99_us=<n> slice=<n> visits=<n> nslots=<n> hist=<edge>:<turns>,...`, p99 and each histogram bucket by its upper edge in microseconds. **The tail by cause**: each turn is classed by what it did -- `publish` (it ended a radar pass and published its rows), `scan` (the patch scan's last row), `decide` (a decision), `plain` (none of them), a turn counted in each class it belongs to -- and the level end logs `impai: tail seat=<i> over_us=1000 publish=<slow>/<turns>:<max>:<mean> scan=… decide=… plain=… worst_us=<n> gametime=<t> publish= scan= decide= slots= visits= cells= flood=`, the slowest turn's counts whole. A slow `plain` turn did only its slice and a bounded radar step, so its time is the OS's (preemption, a fault), not its work's. A measurement, never a verdict: the gate judges the counts of the `work` line. The test lever `tagpu_impai_budget.on` raises the slice to the design point's 15 001 slots and the radar pass to 10^6 visits a tick, to measure each loop's cost per unit of work. The measured figures and the budgets they set: [ImpAI for the player](../impai/player.html), *Cost*.

## The seat table

`s_seat[10]` is `SIM_STATE`, keyed by player record.

- **The level init** (`tagpu_impai_level_init`), on the loader thread inside `0x4919C8`, right after the engine's players init `0x464990`, clears the table on every level, live or puppet. On a live level it marks each record that is seated with controller 2 and whose `seatai` bit is set, logging `impai: seats 1,2 (impure.cfg) of 4 records` (or `seats none`); a record with controller 3 (a network game) marks none and logs why. On a puppet level it marks nothing: the recording restores the table at the level's first step, on the game thread, and between the two clears the recorder is its only writer. A recording that carries no row of the table leaves it clear.
- **The level end** (`tagpu_impai_level_end`), on the game thread in the teardown `0x491B60`'s entry, after the recorder has closed the level's recording: it logs `impai: calls seat=<i> n=<turns> gametime=<t>` for each marked seat, `impai: handicap rows scaled/exempt gametime=<t>: 1=<s>/<e> … 18=<s>/<e>` (the rows with a site: 15 and 16 are absent), the observation's `work`, `cost`, `tail` and `check` lines (*What a seat knows*, *The cost*) and the brain's `eco` and `work` lines (*The brain*), and **clears the table**. So nothing between two levels reads a mark: the next load's loader thread, the only thread that can reach a site before that load's level init (its early pump `0x49727D`, in a network load), is created afterwards by the game thread (`0x4982CA`).
- **Why a game-thread reader sees the whole table**: the level init runs before the loader's last store `0x497C62`, and the game thread runs no step until it has read that store's bit (`0x498342`), the ordering `tagpu_replay.c`'s *THE VIEWER'S BUFFERS* rests on.
- **The recording's header** says which AI played each seat: `RT_PLAYER`'s byte 15 is `tagpu_impai_seat_ai(i)`, 1 when the record is active, controller 2 and marked, else 0 — also in a recording made before the seat choice. The reader refuses a byte above 1. It is written at the header's first capture, after the level init, or at a fork's after the takeover's restore.

## The handicap exemption

Fifteen of the seventeen Difficulty type tests and the on-hit commander purge `0x406FC3` ([sim](../engine/sim.html), *Difficulty and the AI handicap*, has the table with each site's unscaled branch U and scaled continuation S) each become a 5-byte `jmp` to a stub of its own (the rest of the site NOPs). A stub:

1. runs the site's own compare, copied from the site, and its `jne` to U, so a record stock does not scale is not scaled;
2. for a type-2 record, bounds it as the seam does: an offset from record 0 below `0xCEE` (the engine's own loop bound, `0x46602D`) that divides by `0x14B`;
3. when the seat table marks the record, increments the row's exempt count and jumps to U; else increments its scaled count and jumps to S.

It restores `EAX`, `ECX` and `EDX`, touches no x87 register (zero to two values are live on the FPU stack at a site; none at rows 13, 14 and 18: `0x464B88` and `0x464CB8` empty it with `fstp st(0)` before a call, and `0x406F80` holds no x87 value) and calls nothing; the `inc` writes only the flags, which are dead at all 32 targets. Seat 2 marked and seat 1 not, a scaled count is an OTA AI seat's and an exempt one an ImpAI seat's. The counts (`s_diag.hits[18][2]`, a pair a row id, so rows 15 and 16 stay 0) are `SIM_PRIVATE` diagnostics, cleared at the level init; nothing in the simulation reads them.

**Rows 15 and 16, the Deathmatch respawn's two grants, stay stock** (`0x465437`, `0x4654A3`): no ImpAI seat can reach them. The respawn `0x4653D9` creates its commander for the controlled player only: the owner its create `0x485F50` is given is the byte `main+0x2A42`, pushed at `0x4653D2` (DISASSEMBLED; [network](../engine/network.html), *The Deathmatch respawn*), and the type test reads that unit's owner. The controlled player is the local human's record, type 1, which the level init never marks (it marks computer seats, controller 2); it becomes type 2 only through the `AI` command, which clears its mark (below). So the exempt branch could never be taken, and the sites are left as stock has them; the row ids keep the numbering of the [sim](../engine/sim.html) table. A stock respawn needs `ActiveCommanderDeath` 2, which only the multiplayer battle room sets.

**The ordering.** The table is written by the level init on the loader thread inside `0x4919C8`, before every loader-thread path that reaches a site after it (`0x497B29`, `0x497B40`, `0x497C10`) and before the game thread's first step; then only by the recording's restore and the `AI` command, both on the game thread between steps, and by the level end's clear. The one path before the level init, the loader's early pump in a network load (a removed player's units destroyed: scout I1 of the ImpAI plan, function-level reachability), reads the table the previous level's end cleared, so every type-2 record takes S there. On a puppet every recorded record is type 3, which the site's own compare sends to U as stock does.

**Writing `main+0x37EEE` around calls instead is rejected**: the sites are reached from the economy pass, transfers (including the network's), reclaim, building and unit death, and no one bracket covers them.

## The `AI` command

`0x416280`, the console's `AI` (row `0x501FD0`, mask 4: cheats), sets the type of the active record its argument names through `0x463C60` at one of two calls: type 1 becomes 2 at `0x4162F4` (`push 2`), types 2 and 3 become 1 at `0x4162FE` (`push 1`). Both calls come to `impai_ai_command`, `__fastcall(rec, edx, type)` (the record in `ECX`, the type on the stack, popped by the callee as `0x463C60`'s `ret 4` does), which clears the record's mark, bounded as above and never on a puppet, logs `impai: the AI command makes record <i> type <t>: ImpAI no longer plays it` when there was one, and calls `0x463C60`. So `AI` never leaves a seat to ImpAI: on an ImpAI seat it makes the seat a human's, and a second `AI` hands it to the stock AI; on a human's seat it hands it to the stock AI, as in stock, even one a takeover played as an ImpAI seat. It runs on the game thread, or on the loader thread through a profile script line in `0x4648E0`, inside the players init `0x464990`, before the level init clears the table and marks the record if `seatai` names it ([recorder](../engine/recorder.html), the `AI` command's entry).
