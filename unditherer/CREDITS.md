# Credits and licences for the training corpus

The learned restorer was trained on 1,548 true-colour textures from three
public sources. **Every one of them is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal**, which places the work in the public domain: copying, modifying,
redistributing, commercial use and — nothing in the deed or the sources' own
pages says otherwise — using the images to train a model are all permitted,
and attribution is not required. The full legal text is in
[`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`](LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt).

We are giving attribution anyway. Nobody was obliged to make this material
free, and the restorer only exists because they did.

Licence terms were checked against each source's own pages on 2026-09-01; the
quotes below are from those pages. The exact list of images is
`corpus/manifest.json`; `corpus/ATTRIBUTION.md` (regenerated by
`python -m unditherer.credits`) names every image, its page and its authors.

## Not in the training set

- **The game's palette** (`ta-palette.json`) is 256 RGB triplets read from the
  frame buffer of Total Annihilation (Cavedog Entertainment, 1997). It is used
  to quantise the training pairs; no game art is used as training data.
- **The fifteen screenshots** in `research/notes/assets/undither/shots/` are
  frames of the game and its expansion The Core Contingency. They are used
  only to evaluate the result and to illustrate the wiki page. They belong to
  the game's rights holders.

## The code

Everything under `unditherer/` was written for this project. The classical
stages follow the restoration handover document quoted in the wiki page; the
learned model is a DnCNN-shaped residual network (Zhang et al., 2017) trained
from scratch on the corpus above.
